Sikhism and the Golden Temple

The Golden Temple (Sri Harmandir Sahib) in Amritsar is the holiest shrine of Sikhism and a symbol of peace, equality, and devotion. Built by Guru Arjan Dev Ji, it welcomes people of all religions and is famous for its golden architecture and the world's largest free community kitchen (Langar). Other important Gurudwaras in Punjab include Takht Sri Kesgarh Sahib, Fatehgarh Sahib, Dukh Niwaran Sahib and Ber Sahib, each holding great historical and spiritual significance in Sikh history.

History OF Jallianwala Bagh

Jallianwala Bagh is a historic memorial located in Amritsar, Punjab, dedicated to the brave men, women, and children who lost their lives in the Jallianwala Bagh Massacre on 13 April 1919. During the peaceful gathering, British troops led by General Reginald Dyer opened fire on thousands of unarmed civilians, killing hundreds and injuring many more. Today, the memorial preserves the bullet-marked walls and the Martyrs' Well, reminding visitors of India's struggle for freedom and inspiring respect for the sacrifices made by countless freedom fighters.

Wagah Border

The Wagah Border, located about 30 km from Amritsar, is the famous international border between India and Pakistan. It is best known for the Beating Retreat Ceremony, held every evening by the Border Security Force (BSF) of India and the Pakistan Rangers. The ceremony features synchronized marching, flag-lowering, and patriotic performances, attracting thousands of visitors from around the world. Wagah Border is a symbol of national pride, discipline, and the shared history of the two nations, making it one of Punjab's most popular tourist attractions..
